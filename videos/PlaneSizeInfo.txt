If you want to play a video on a 3D plane mesh, start by creating a plane and make sure its aspect ratio matches the resolution of the video—for example, if your video is 1080 x 1080 pixels, use a square plane. Do not adjust the size by manipulating vertices or edges. Instead, use the default scale tools to resize the plane. Set the scale of the plane to 100 x 100 units. Then, rotate the plane 90 degrees on the X-axis and 180 degrees on the Y-axis. After making these adjustments, freeze the transformations to reset the transform values. This setup ensures the video maps correctly onto the 3D plane without distortion.
